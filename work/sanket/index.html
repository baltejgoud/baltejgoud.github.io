01 / The challenge

From business data to a clearer next move.

Businesses have to buy, stock and plan before demand is certain. A forecast alone does not tell a team what to do next. Sanket brings that forecast into the same workspace as inventory, risk and the decisions waiting for attention.

My contribution

I lead Sanket as founder and CEO, bringing product direction and engineering together. The platform connects a React dashboard, a FastAPI gateway and a forecasting service, with tenant-separated data in PostgreSQL.

Built with

React · TypeScript · FastAPI · PostgreSQL · ML forecasting

02 / Decisions behind the interfaceProduct meets engineering

The details
do the work.

01

Start with the decision

The dashboard puts decisions and operational signals ahead of a wall of charts. The aim is to connect the evidence to a practical action: replenish, maintain stock or investigate risk.

02

Show a range, not a promise

SKU forecast views show expected demand alongside an uncertainty band. A planning interface should make uncertainty visible, so a precise-looking number does not become false confidence.

03

Account for different businesses

Fashion, electronics, pharma and agro have different planning needs. Industry workspaces and model routing let the same platform handle seasonal demand, product lifecycles and cold-start products.

03 / In the product

The forecast becomes part of the workflow.

Each SKU carries its own demand curve, expected range and planning context. These captures show the actual development product using sandbox records.

04 / Where it stands

A product being built, with a business behind it.

The development product connects industry workspaces, SKU-level forecasts and inventory planning views. The captures show how planning signals become reviewable decisions, using sandbox records. My next focus is validating the workflow with the businesses it is designed to serve.
